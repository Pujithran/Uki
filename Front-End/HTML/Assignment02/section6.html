Comfort Meets Craftmanship
Embodying the raw, wayward spirit of Scandinavian design, the Asgaard sofa was created to invite quite conversation.
Every joint is hand-finished, every seam is double-stitched, and every cushion is filled to hold its shape through years of
daily use.

Available is three sizes and a range of upholstery colours, this piece adapts to apartment and large living rooms alike,
without losing its distinct silhoutte.
